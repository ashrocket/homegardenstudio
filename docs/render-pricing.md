# Rendering credit and changing inference costs

Purchased credit is HomeGardenStudio spending value. Each cloud rendering has a displayed price that the customer approves before submission. Future prices can change; the service must honor the disclosed debit of an accepted, valid quote. Credit does not promise a fixed number of images or a fixed quantity of provider tokens.

The launch promotion is $10 for $15 of credit, available for 90 days from explicit activation. The offer deadline closes new sales. It does not expire existing credit or prevent verification of a legitimate earlier or delayed transaction. There is no automatic renewal. Confirm the exact store price and close App Store product availability consistently with the app's offer window.

Before enabling a model, verify its input/output pricing, image dimensions, quality and actual billed usage. Configure a retail rendering price that covers the credit discount, store commission, taxes or adjustments and operating costs. For a credit grant `G`, net purchase proceeds `N`, and expected provider cost `C`, the break-even retail debit is at least `C × G / N`, before other costs and margin. The initial app-unlock credit grants also need a funded acquisition budget; discounted top-ups do not fund them automatically.

For illustration, a $10 sale at a 15% store commission leaves $8.50 before other deductions. A $15 grant therefore needs about 1.77 times provider cost in retail debit just to cover that promotion. The account's actual commission and proceeds must be checked; eligibility for [Apple's Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) is not assumed.

If a model's provider cost triples, either reprice future quotes, offer a lower-cost option with the user's choice, or disable that model until its rates are verified. Do not silently substitute a provider or change a submitted request. The current operator ceiling counts quoted managed usage, including failed attempts; it is a conservative budget control, not a guarantee about the provider's invoice after an unexpected price change.

Keep managed generation off until price/funding verification is complete. Local editing and native previews remain available without credit. User spending caps never purchase credit. Apple's [in-app purchase rule](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase) requires purchased credits not to expire.
