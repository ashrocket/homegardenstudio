# Support

**Development documentation.** The workflows and US prices below describe the planned first release. The app is not yet available through TestFlight or the App Store.

HomeGardenStudio is a garden planner for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Plans, imports and previews work on your device; cloud renderings are optional.

## Getting help

Open an issue at https://github.com/ashrocket/homegardenstudio/issues and say which device and app version you're using. Please don't post photos of your home, your address or any provider keys.

## Common questions

**What does the free version include?** Every view and tool, with up to 12 features per plan. Plans you've made stay openable, editable and exportable whether or not you unlock.

**What do unlocks cost?** iPhone and iPad unlock: US $1.99, including $2 of rendering credit. Mac unlock: US $3.99, including $2 of rendering credit. Both platforms: US $4.99, including $5 of rendering credit. If you already own the iPhone and iPad unlock, the upgrade to both platforms is US $3.00; if you already own the Mac unlock, it's US $0.99. Either upgrade includes $5 of rendering credit. Restoring purchases never adds credit a second time.

**How much does a cloud rendering cost?** The app shows the exact debit before you confirm each rendering. Local previews never use credit.

**Is a cloud rendering a measured plan?** No. A rendering is an AI concept based on your layout and photo. A photo's two-point scale helps with approximate placement; perspective can still distort distances. Only dimensions or positions you explicitly verify through field measurement are labelled measured. Product dimensions keep their listing provenance. Editing geometry or removing its supporting source can invalidate that certainty.

**What happens after the included credit runs out?** Local planning remains available. You can choose additional prepaid credit with no automatic renewal, your own provider key, or an available funded sponsorship. An optional monthly spending cap limits how much existing credit you use; raising the cap never charges you. Additional credit products and prices are not yet configured. No sponsorship is offered without approved inventory and a finite funded allowance.
