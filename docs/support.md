# Support

HomeGardenStudio is a garden planner for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Plans, imports and previews work on your device; cloud renderings are optional.

## Getting help

Open an issue at https://github.com/ashrocket/homegardenstudio/issues and say which device and app version you're using. Please don't post photos of your home, your address or any provider keys.

## Common questions

**What does the free version include?** Every view and tool, with up to 12 features per plan. Plans you've made stay openable, editable and exportable whether or not you unlock.

**What do unlocks cost?** iPhone and iPad unlock: US $1.99, including $2 of rendering credit. Mac unlock: US $3.99, including $2 of rendering credit. Both platforms: US $4.99, including $5 of rendering credit. If you already own the iPhone and iPad unlock, the upgrade to both platforms is US $3.00; if you already own the Mac unlock, it's US $0.99. Either upgrade includes $5 of rendering credit. Restoring purchases never adds credit a second time.

**How much does a cloud rendering cost?** The app shows the exact debit before you confirm each rendering. Local previews never use credit.

**Is a cloud rendering a measured plan?** No. A rendering is an AI concept based on your layout and photo. Measurements come only from the dimensions you enter and from photos you've calibrated.
